# Editorial message templates

Standard messages for handling editors to post on submission issues. Adapt them to each case; replace everything in [brackets].

## 1. Acknowledgement

Thank you for your submission to Examen Silentii. I am [name], and I will be the handling editor. I will check that the submission is within scope and meets the requirements, and reply within two weeks.

## 2. Out of scope or incomplete

Thank you for your submission. I am unable to send it for review because [it falls outside the journal's scope / it does not yet meet the following requirements: ...]. [If the problem can be fixed: You are welcome to revise and resubmit as a new issue once ...]

## 3. Invitation to review

@[reviewer], would you be willing to review this submission? Reviewers rerun the authors' test from the archived materials and complete the [reviewer checklist](REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md) in a comment here, ideally within four weeks. Please first confirm that you have no conflict of interest, or declare it.

## 4. Invitation to maintainers

@[maintainer], this submission examines [software or dataset]. You are welcome to comment on this issue at any point, and to submit a formal response, which would be published alongside the article. You have at least 30 days to do so; please let me know if you need longer. See the [guide for maintainers](https://examen-silentii.github.io/maintainers/).

## 5. Revisions requested

Thank you to the reviewers. Based on their reports, I am asking for [minor / major] revisions. Please address each point raised, and reply here with a summary of your changes and links to the revised manuscript and materials.

## 6. Acceptance

I am pleased to accept this submission for publication in Examen Silentii. I will now archive the article on Zenodo and confirm its DOI here. [The maintainers' response, if received, will be published alongside it.]

## 7. Rejection

Thank you for your submission. After considering the reviews, I am unable to accept it, because [reasons]. You may appeal this decision once, by stating your reasons here within 30 days.

## 8. Publication

This article has been published as *Examen Silentii*, Volume [N], Article [N]: https://doi.org/[DOI]. Thank you to the authors, reviewers [and maintainers]. This issue will now be closed; it remains public as the record of the review.
