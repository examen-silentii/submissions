# Reviewer checklist

Reviewers copy this checklist into a comment on the submission issue and complete it. Each item should be ticked only when it has been checked, with notes where needed.

## Conflicts of interest

- [ ] I have no conflict of interest with the authors, the software or data examined, or its maintainers, or I have declared it here:

## Requirements

- [ ] **Subject.** The software or dataset, and the exact version tested, are clearly identified.
- [ ] **Significance.** The software or dataset is widely used, or an error in it would have consequences.
- [ ] **Reproducibility.** I reran the test from the archived materials, following the authors' instructions.
  - Environment used (operating system, versions):
  - Did my results match the authors'? Note any differences:
- [ ] **Independent reference.** The reference is independent of what was tested, and any shared code, methods or ancestry is declared.
- [ ] **Findings.** The claims follow from the results, and their size and limits are stated accurately.
- [ ] **Signalling.** The article states correctly whether the software or dataset signalled the problem.
- [ ] **Maintainers.** The maintainers were notified before submission, and their response, or its absence, is reported fairly.
- [ ] **Security.** If the problem could be a security vulnerability, the project's security policy was followed.

## Presentation

- [ ] The article is clear, and a reader could follow and repeat the test.
- [ ] References and links are complete and working.

- [ ] Accept
- [ ] Minor revisions
- [ ] Major revisions
- [ ] Reject
